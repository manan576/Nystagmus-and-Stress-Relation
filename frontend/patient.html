How to Use the Patient Studio

Step-by-step clinical protocol for recording trial telemetry

1
Connect Telemetry: Pair your Polar H9 chest strap via Web Bluetooth, or start the simulated stream for testing.
2
Start New Session: Click + Start New Session. Sit quietly for the 5-minute baseline calibration (calculates resting median RMSSD).
3
Automatic Video Capture: When a head oscillation occurs, a 10s video (5s pre + 5s post) is recorded and securely saved.
4
Neuro-Cognitive Stress Test: Take the 60s Stroop test (hotkeys 1-4) to measure vagal withdrawal and tremor worsening.
5
Finalize Session: Click End Active Session to disconnect hardware, lock data, and sync to the clinician portal.
